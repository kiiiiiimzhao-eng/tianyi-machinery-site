Key Features

High Screening Efficiency

Optimized vibration parameters and screen mesh design ensure high separation accuracy and throughput across all mesh sizes.

Adjustable Vibration Amplitude

Easily adjustable eccentric block or exciter settings to fine-tune amplitude for different material characteristics and capacities.

Wear-Resistant Screen Mesh

High-tensile woven wire, polyurethane, and rubber screen panels available. Quick-release tensioning system for fast mesh changes.

Modular Deck Design

1 to 4 deck configurations for multi-stage classification. Each deck can use different mesh sizes for precise particle separation.

Low Noise Operation

Rubber spring suspension, balanced exciter design, and optional acoustic enclosures reduce operating noise below 85 dB(A).

Enclosed Options for Dust Control

Fully enclosed screen bodies with dust extraction ports available for environmentally sensitive or indoor applications.

Typical Applications

Mining Ore Classification

Primary and secondary screening of iron ore, copper ore, gold ore, and other minerals after crushing stages.

Sand & Gravel Grading

Multi-deck classification of aggregates into specific size fractions for concrete, asphalt, and construction applications.

Coal Preparation

Raw coal sizing, desliming, and product classification in coal washing and processing plants.

Chemical Powder Screening

Fine mesh screening of chemical powders, fertilizers, and industrial minerals with enclosed dust-proof designs.

Recycling & Waste Processing

Trommel and vibrating screens for sorting construction waste, municipal solid waste, and recycled aggregate materials.

Quarry & Aggregate Processing

Scalping and finished product screening in stone crushing plants with high-wear urethane screen panels.
