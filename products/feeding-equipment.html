Key Features

Uniform Feeding Rate

Precise, consistent material discharge ensures stable downstream process operation. Adjustable amplitude, frequency, or gate opening for fine control.

Variable Frequency Drive Control

VFD integration allows instant feed rate adjustment from the control room. Remote start/stop and speed regulation for automated production lines.

Wear-Resistant Liners

Replaceable wear liners in high-abrasion zones extend equipment service life. Options include hardened steel, polyurethane, and ceramic tiles.

Sealed Designs for Dust Control

Enclosed hopper interfaces and sealed discharge chutes minimize fugitive dust emissions, improving workplace safety and environmental compliance.

Integration with Upstream/Downstream Equipment

Standardized flanges and interfaces for seamless connection with silos, hoppers, conveyors, crushers, and screens in complete material handling systems.

Multiple Feeder Types

Choose from vibrating, electromagnetic vibrating, rotary vane, screw, or belt feeders to match your material characteristics and process requirements.

Typical Applications

Mining Hopper Discharge

Heavy-duty vibrating feeders for controlled extraction of run-of-mine ore from primary hoppers and surge bins.

Silo Discharge

Rotary vane feeders and screw feeders for reliable, flood-fed discharge from storage silos in cement, grain, and chemical plants.

Batching Systems

Precision belt feeders and screw feeders for weigh-batching of aggregates, powders, and additives in concrete and asphalt plants.

Crusher Feeding

Vibrating grizzly feeders with integral scalping bars to pre-screen fines and provide controlled feed to jaw, cone, and impact crushers.

Packaging Lines

Screw and rotary vane feeders for accurate bag filling and bulk bag loading in cement, fertilizer, and chemical packaging operations.

Biomass & Alternative Fuels

Specialized feeders for irregular, fibrous, and bridging-prone materials such as wood chips, RDF, and agricultural residues.
