Every idler, sprocket, bucket, and chain is designed specifically for our product range — belt conveyors, chain conveyors, bucket elevators, and screw conveyors.

Our engineering team helps identify the correct replacement part from equipment serial numbers, photos, or dimensional sketches. Expert advice on upgrade alternatives.

Typical Applications

Belt Conveyor Parts

Idlers (trough, impact, return, self-aligning), pulleys (drive, tail, bend, take-up), belt cleaners, skirt rubber, and conveyor belts.

Chain Conveyor Parts

Drop-forged scraper chains, drive sprockets, tail sprockets, guide rails, wear strips, and tensioning assemblies.

Bucket Elevator Parts

Elevator buckets (plastic and steel), belt-type and chain-type bucket attachments, pulleys, sprockets, and elevator belting.

Screw Conveyor Parts

Screw flights and augers, trough liners, hanger bearings, end bearings, seals, and shaft couplings.

Apron Conveyor Parts

Overlapping steel pans, drive chains, track rollers, sprockets, and support rails for heavy-duty apron feeders and pan conveyors.

General Wear Parts

Ceramic and bi-metallic wear liners, chute liners, impact bars, belt scrapers, and dust seals for all conveyor types.
