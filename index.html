About Us

China's Leading Bulk Material Conveyor Manufacturer

Hubei Tianyi Machinery Co., Ltd. (Stock Code: 832654) was founded in 2007 and is headquartered in Yidu City, Hubei Province, within the convenient "half-hour economic circle" of Yichang. Our 70,000-square-meter manufacturing facility is home to over 200 professionals dedicated to delivering world-class bulk material handling equipment.

We hold 100+ national patents (including 13 invention patents) and 7 software copyrights. As a member of the Conveyor Feeder Branch of the China Heavy Machinery Industry Association, we have led and participated in drafting 3 national standards and 5 industry standards.

Our equipment is not only popular across China but has been exported to Russia, Indonesia, Vietnam, Thailand, Mongolia, India, Mexico, Argentina, Congo, Ethiopia, and many more regions. Our professional after-sales team provides year-round global service from technical consultation to installation and commissioning.

Our Products

Complete Range of Bulk Material Handling Equipment

From single equipment to complete conveying systems, we provide customized solutions for non-ferrous metal smelting, thermal power, chemical, environmental protection, waste-to-energy, and biomass industries.

Belt Conveyor

Belt Conveyor

Long-distance, large-capacity belt conveyors for power plants, mines, ports, and chemical plants. Available in semi-enclosed and open configurations with capacities up to 2,000 t/h.
☰
Chain Conveyor

Chain Conveyor

Universal en-masse chain conveyors including horizontal and vertical types. Uses scraper chains in closed casings for reliable bulk material transport. Ideal for abrasive and high-temperature materials.
☰
Bucket Elevator

Bucket Elevator

High-throughput vertical conveying equipment featuring stable operation and long service life. TH Series and TD Series available for grain, cement, coal, and mineral applications.
☰
Screw Conveyor

Screw Conveyor

Continuous conveying without traction components. Uses rotating spiral blades to move powder, granular, and small lump materials in sealed pipelines. Dust-free, space-efficient design.
☰
Apron Conveyor

Apron Conveyor

Heavy-duty equipment for transporting bulk materials in open rectangular plates. Designed for high-temperature, heavy, and abrasive materials. BLT chain apron conveyor series available.
☰
Dust Collector

Industrial Dust Collector

PPCS bag dust collectors for multi-phase separation of gas and dust particles. Essential for environmentally-compliant production in cement, chemical, and metallurgical industries.
☰
Feeding Equipment

Feeding Equipment

Vibrating feeders, rotary feeders, and screw feeders for controlled, uniform material feeding into conveying and processing systems. Adjustable feed rates for precise batching.
☰
Crushing Equipment

Crushing Equipment

Jaw crushers, hammer crushers, and roll crushers for reducing bulk material size before conveying. Integrated solutions for crushing and conveying in one system.
☰
Screening Equipment

Screening Equipment

Vibrating screens and rotary screens for grading and separating bulk materials by particle size. Essential for quality control in mining, chemical, and construction material processing.
☰
Valves

Valves

Complete range of pneumatic, electric, and manual valves: plug-in valves, flap valves, roller gates, three-way and four-way diverter valves for bulk material flow control in conveying systems.

Non-Ferrous Metal Smelting Conveyor System

Thermal Power Plant Ash Handling System

Full ash handling and slag conveying solutions for coal-fired power plants including submerged scraper conveyors, belt conveyors, and dust collection systems with continuous operation capability.

Waste-to-Energy Plant Conveying System

Comprehensive bulk material handling system for an overseas waste-to-energy facility. Includes apron conveyors, chain conveyors, and dust collectors tailored for MSW-derived fuel transport.
