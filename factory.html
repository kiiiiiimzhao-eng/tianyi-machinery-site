Production Facilities

Our factory is equipped with advanced CNC machining centers, laser cutting machines, automated welding lines, and a dedicated testing and quality assurance center. Every conveyor system undergoes rigorous inspection before leaving our facility.

CNC Machining Center

⚙

Steel Structure Workshop

⚙

Assembly & Testing Workshop

Full-scale assembly and no-load testing of complete conveyor systems before disassembly and shipping.

Quality Control Lab

Material testing, dimensional inspection, dynamic balancing, and NDT (non-destructive testing) capabilities.

Quality Commitment

As a member of the Conveyor Feeder Branch of the China Heavy Machinery Industry Association, we have participated in drafting 3 national standards and 5 industry standards. Our quality management system is certified to ISO standards, and every piece of equipment is tested to meet design specifications before delivery.

We welcome customers to visit our factory to see our production capabilities firsthand. Our team can arrange factory tours, technical discussions, and on-site equipment demonstrations.
